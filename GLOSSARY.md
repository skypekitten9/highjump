# Highjump

A first-person movement game built around vaulting with a pole, alongside other movement abilities like wall running and sliding.

## Language

**Vault**:
A jump in which the player plants a **Pole** and uses it to launch upward and over.
_Avoid_: High jump, pole jump

**Pole**:
The long, flexible pole the player carries and plants to perform a **Vault**.
_Avoid_: Stick, staff
